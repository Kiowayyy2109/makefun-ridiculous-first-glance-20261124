Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a): a program that writes one sentence, at most 200 characters, pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would sell.

## The pitch

> We bottle the air from the exact spot where celebrities sneezed and sell it to collectors, because fans already pay thousands for stars' trash and air is the only souvenir nobody can fake.

188 characters, one sentence. Fans demonstrably pay for celebrity memorabilia, and air is the one souvenir nobody can fake — the absurdity is the moat.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, fully deterministic) validates the pitch — 1–200 characters, exactly one sentence, single line — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
